Sam Altman: OpenAI now writes explicit safety cases before frontier RL runs

Altman posted that OpenAI formulates explicit safety cases in advance of frontier reinforcement learning runs expected to significantly increase capability. He welcomes a federal framework for consistent safety requirements but says labs need not wait for legislation. Pacing means slower than maximum speed so alignment stays ahead of capabilities; progress continues. Why it matters: shifts the public safety conversation from post-training release to pre-run development controls at the current frontier lab. Watch next: whether Anthropic, Google DeepMind or xAI adopt comparable pre-run safety cases and publish them.
